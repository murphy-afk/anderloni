The color of the light

Photograhs of white surfaces exposed during different hours of the day and at different geographical points of reference. During a journey of aproximately 2.000 km I've stopped with regular intervals and taken photos of the white hood of the car in which I was travelling. Thus, the sunsets, the nightfalls, the highlights of the day, sunshines and cloudy weather were registred, alongside the neon signs from the gas station, from the street lamps…

El color de la luz

Fotografías de superficies blancas expuestas a diferentes horas del dia y en diferentes lugares geograficos. Durante un viaje de cerca de 2.000 Km. me he parado a intervalos regulares y he sacado fotografias del capó del coche (blanco) con el que viajaba. Así han quedado registrados amaneceres, noches, mediodias de sol o nubes, rotulos luminosos de gasolineras, luz de farolas …
